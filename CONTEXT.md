# Household glossary

- **Resident**: a selectable household member with personal needs and an action queue.
- **Need**: hunger, energy, bladder, hygiene, fun or social satisfaction, measured from 0 to 100.
- **Action queue**: a resident's ordered intentions, including the current action. Cancellation removes an intention immediately.
- **Interaction**: a timed activity performed after walking to an adjacent accessible position.
- **Autonomy**: an idle resident choosing care for their lowest need without a player command.
- **Friendship**: the shared relationship between the two housemates.
- **Reading nook**: a purchasable furnishing used for leisure.
