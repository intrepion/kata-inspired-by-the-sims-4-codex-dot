WELCOME HOME

A day, at your own pace.

Select Mira or Rowan. Click furniture in the home and choose its interaction. Your housemate walks there before using it. Click open floor to walk.

Actions join the selected person's queue; cancel any with ×. Needs fall over time. Idle housemates automatically look after a need below 35. Friendly chats restore both people's social need and grow their friendship.

Camera: Q / E or ↶ / ↷ rotate, scroll or ＋ / − zoom. Arrow keys pan. ⌂ View resets. Time: Space or 0 pauses; 1 / 2 / 3 set speed.

Buy: pauses the home; finish or cancel all queues, then click a free floor square to place a 90-credit reading nook. Escape exits. Essential paths must stay open.

Meals cost 8. Start with 420 credits. Save manually or let autosave run every 10 seconds. Progress stays in this browser. No game over: this is a small household sandbox.
